Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access the documents? It mentions a zONE?

Many of the Business Service Documents (BSDs) and technical specifications are hosted on the CMS zONE portal. To access these, you must have an active CMS zONE account.

Instructions to access CMS zONE:

  1. Visit the CMS zONE Portal.
  2. If you don't have an account, you will need to register. Click on the "Register" link on the portal home page.
  3. Follow the registration instructions, which may require a CMS Enterprise Identity Management (EIDM) or HCQIS Access Roles and Profile (HARP) account depending on your role.
  4. Once registered for the zONE, request access to the "CMS FDSH Services Repository" and "FDSH State Testing" communities.
  5. Once registered and logged in, you can search for the specific FDSH documents or use the links provided in this tool.
